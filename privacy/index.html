Privacy Policy

IntervalPro for Android · Last updated 1 January 2026

IntervalPro is an interval timer. It does not have user accounts, and it does not ask for your name, email address or any other personal details.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-up, no profile.
  • Your timer settings and last session are stored only on your own device.
  • The app shows advertising through Google AdMob, which uses device identifiers to serve ads.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDK is included in the app.

What the app stores on your device

Your timer settings (prep, work, rest and rounds for each block), any saved presets, your sound preference, and a snapshot of the current session — so a running timer survives the app being closed — are stored locally in the app's private storage. They are not uploaded anywhere, and they are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Advertising

The app displays a banner advertisement supplied by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and process a device advertising identifier, IP address, and coarse device information. This processing is governed by Google's own policies.

  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out of personalised advertising, in Android's Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app asks for your consent before the first ad request, using Google's consent management form. You can change that choice by clearing the app's data.
  • See how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services.

Purchases

The optional one-time "Remove ads" purchase is processed by Google Play Billing. IntervalPro never receives or stores your payment details; it only records whether the purchase is owned, via Google Play.

This website

This site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub logs the IP address of visitors for security purposes, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. The site itself sets no cookies, runs no JavaScript, and includes no third-party trackers, fonts or analytics.

Children

IntervalPro is a training tool intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
