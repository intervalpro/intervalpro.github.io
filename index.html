Built to stay out of the way

Six things that matter once you are mid-round, breathing hard, and not looking at a settings screen.

A clock that never drifts

Every frame is calculated from the system's monotonic clock rather than counting ticks. A throttled phone or a 40-minute screen-off cannot make a round run long.

Prep, work, rest — colour coded

A prep countdown so you are set before round one, a green work phase, a red rest phase. You read the phase from across the room without reading any text.

Chain real sessions

Stack blocks — four rounds of 1:00/0:30, then six of 0:40/0:20. The prep runs once, then each block follows the last with no manual restart.

Save the workout, load it later

Save a session as a preset and load it back next time, instead of re-tuning every picker before you start. Your last-used values are kept too.

Keeps running in the background

A live countdown notification in the shade, so you can check the round with the phone in your pocket. The timer is a foreground service — not a screen you must keep open.

Big digits, either orientation

The countdown scales to the display, so landscape on a stand is as readable as portrait in your hand. Your screen stays awake while the session runs.

Questions

Does the timer keep running when the screen is off?

Yes. The session runs in an Android foreground service with a live countdown in the notification shade, so locking the phone or switching apps does not stop it. There is also an option to keep the screen awake while you train.

What happens if I close the app mid-session?

Nothing. The session is restored when you come back — round, phase and remaining time intact — and a paused session resumes with exactly the time that was left.

What are prep, work and rest?

Prep is the countdown before round one, so you can set your feet. Work is the effort interval, rest is the recovery. Prep runs once at the start; work and rest alternate for every round of the block.

Can I build a session with different intervals?

Yes. A session is a list of blocks, each with its own work, rest and rounds. Four rounds of 1:00/0:30 followed by six of 0:40/0:20 is one session, started once.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No account, and no connection needed to train. Your settings and last session are stored on the device.

Which Android versions are supported?

Android 8.0 and newer, on phones and tablets. The layout adapts to portrait and landscape.
